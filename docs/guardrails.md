# Workshop guardrails

- Use only fictional/synthetic records. No PHI, real names, customer identifiers,
  production documents, credentials or sensitive screenshots in the delivery
  system, including Copilot prompts, PRs and logs.
- Fictional age bands and policy thresholds are teaching rules, not legal or
  clinical guidance. Do not transplant them into production without review.
- Humans approve behavior, plans, exceptions, PRs and merges. AI-generated
  content is a draft; no automatic agent merge is part of this workshop.
- Unknown privacy facts deny visibility. A routing classification is not a
  claim, authorization, appeal or grievance decision.
- Exact-match and authenticated flags are synthetic inputs. They do not prove
  identity, reviewer permissions, audit persistence or integration safety.
- No dependencies or remote integrations beyond the documented npm toolchain
  are required. Review additional extensions before installation.
- Organization administrators own platform access, data-processing agreements
  and required review policies. This repository does not certify any service
  or deployment for regulated data.
- The coverage checker is not a sensitive-data detector. Inspect examples,
  prompts and screenshots; use approved secret/data controls for real work.

The teaching code keeps no external records and should never be pointed at
production systems. Consult your own compliance and privacy teams for real use.
