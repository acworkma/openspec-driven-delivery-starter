# Full-day agenda

**Audience:** product owners, architects, developers and leaders in mixed-role pairs
or small teams. **Length:** eight hours, including breaks and lunch.
The allocations below are teaching blocks, not estimates of engineering effort.

| Segment | Min | # | Learning objective and output |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Welcome, goals, and the method in one picture | 20 | 1 | Intent, spec, plan, tasks, human gates and traceability; AI drafts, humans decide |
| Loop demo on case 003 | 35 | 2 | Trace the triage brief through proposal, Given/When/Then, tasks and four gates; stop on missing behavior and assumptions |
| Retro-spec drill (case 004) | 40 | 3 | Expose missing rules and dependencies before seeing the clarified answer |
| Break | 15 | 4 | |
| Lab 001, part 1: intent → OpenSpec change | 55 | 5 | Clarify and draft the bounded proposal and requirement delta |
| Lunch | 60 | 6 | |
| Lab 001, part 2: design, Given/When/Then, tasks | 45 | 7 | Review scenarios, assumptions, dependencies, synthetic test data and a bounded plan |
| Lab 001, part 3: implement with Copilot | 60 | 8 | Implement the policy and inspect evidence; use reference catch-up if needed |
| Break | 15 | 9 | |
| CI, PR review and archive (combined) | 35 | 10 | Inspect hosted checks, independent review and the maintained contract; name incomplete steps |
| Your first spec (pairs) | 70 | 11 | Draft and challenge your own candidate spec, complete the assumptions/dependencies/test-data section and pick 1-2 baseline measures |
| Readouts and next steps | 30 | 12 | Share the three outputs and what we'll take to Azure Boards / our repo next week |
| Total | 480 | | One on-site day, including lunch and two breaks |

Use the [facilitator guide](facilitator-guide.md) to run each block.
The main take-home is [your first spec](first-spec.md), not a finished production
application. Only [lab 001](lab-001.md) involves participant coding; product
owners and architects can finish every other segment without writing code.
Case 003 opens the day, 004 is the retro, 001 is the bounded delivery lab,
and 002 remains [optional self-study](cases/README.md#optional-self-study-002-dental).
